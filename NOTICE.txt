NOTICES AND INFORMATION
Do Not Translate or Localize

This project incorporates third-party open source software. Complete license
and attribution text is generated for each release surface from the dependencies
actually redistributed by that artifact:

- Brewlet CLI archives: NOTICE.txt beside the executable.
- Operator and admission container images: /NOTICE.txt.
- Node provisioner container image: /NOTICE.txt.
- Brewlet Maven plugin: META-INF/NOTICE.txt.
- Brewlet website and generated documentation: /NOTICE.txt.

The generation and verification process is documented in
docs/third-party-notices.md and enforced by the repository's CI and release
workflows. The checked-in website and Maven plugin notices are available at
site/NOTICE.txt and maven-plugin/src/main/resources/META-INF/NOTICE.txt.

Container images also include Microsoft's container legal notice at
/CONTAINER-LEGAL-NOTICE.txt. Third-party license files retained in base images
remain in their original filesystem locations.
