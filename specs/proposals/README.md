# Brewlet design proposals

Substantial changes to the [Brewlet specification](../SPECIFICATION.md)
begin here as numbered, reviewable proposals. A proposal is not a current
contract; once approved and implemented, its requirements are merged into the
specification and the proposal file is removed. Committed history retains it.

There are no open proposals. Approved Proposals 0009 (language-agnostic runtime
images) and 0010 (governed dependency composition) now form Parts I and II of
the specification. New proposals continue the numbering after 0010.

## Deferred research

Stronger sandbox isolation and stock-runtime baked export remain possible
future directions, not supported alternatives or committed roadmap features.
Their boundaries are recorded briefly in the
[specification's open questions](../SPECIFICATION.md#14-open-questions).
